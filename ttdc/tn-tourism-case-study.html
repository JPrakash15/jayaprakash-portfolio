UI / UX Case Study

Redesigning Tamil Nadu Tourism into a website people actually want to explore.

A theme-based, mobile-first redesign built around real traveler stories, transparent booking, and the state's own cultural identity - not a government-notice-board.

Why this matters to the department

Business goals

User research pointed to real business stakes - a redesign only earns its budget if it moves these too.

GOAL 01

Convert visits into direct bookings

Verified listings keep travelers booking through the government site instead of leaking to OTAs like MakeMyTrip.

↑ booking conversion
GOAL 02

Cut bounce and drive return visits

Interviewees described the old site as unusable on mobile - a clearer structure keeps them exploring instead of leaving.

↓ bounce rate
GOAL 03

Spread tourism revenue beyond hotspots

Theme-based discovery surfaces lesser-known temples, hills, and coastal towns - not just the destinations everyone already knows.

↑ destination reach
GOAL 04

Build trust a government platform can own

Travelers said they don't trust official sites the way they trust private platforms - real stories and photos close that gap.

↑ platform credibility

Where the market falls short

Who this is for

User personas

Three personas structured the platform around the tour organizer and the traveler - not the department publishing the content.

The IT Professional

26 yrs · loves weekend travel
Goal
Easily find destinations that suit family interests - temples, hill stations, and more.
Motivation
Create lasting memories with his family through safe, enjoyable travel.
Pain point
Difficulty navigating the old Tamil Nadu Tourism website on mobile.
Happiness
A clean, user-friendly layout with clear travel themes.

The Traveller

34 yrs · loves to travel
Goal
Access sanitized and safe accommodation options.
Motivation
Discover hidden destinations and authentic local experiences in Tamil Nadu.
Pain point
Lack of filters or themed recommendations - content felt generic.
Happiness
Family-friendly features like "top activities for kids" and weekend family packages.
What each traveler needs

User stories

Twelve stories were written to pin down what situation, and for what purpose, the platform would be used.

Solo adventure seeker

I want to discover adventurous places like trekking and surfing spots, so I can plan an exciting trip without existing trip experiences.

Family traveler

I want to explore destinations that are safe and family-friendly, so I can plan a trip without worrying about logistics.

Cultural tourist

I want to find pilgrimage destinations and cultural events, so I can plan my trip during important festivals.

Visual learner

I want to read real traveler stories and see actual photos, so I can feel confident and inspired before booking.

Betting on a fix

Page hypotheses

Each hypothesis links a design decision to a problem found in research - so every screen ships with a reason attached.

If

we organize website content around user intent and travel themes

Then

users will find relevant destinations, activities, and accommodations faster

Because

they aren't currently able to filter or explore based on specific interests like adventure, honeymoon, or religious tourism on the site

If

we let the site offer booking or inquiries through verified listings

Then

travelers will avoid third-party uncertainty and prefer using the government site

Because

trust in authenticity and safety is a major concern

If

we show real traveler stories and photos

Then

users will feel more confident exploring lesser-known destinations

Because

social proof builds trust and inspires exploration

If

we introduce a clear, mobile-friendly navigation structure

Then

engagement and return visits will increase

Because

the current bounce rate suggests users leave due to poor usability

From structure to shipped screen

Final design

The homepage carries every research decision through - theme-based browsing, a persistent booking widget, real traveler stories, and a trust strip built to compete with private platforms.

01

Persistent booking widget

Hotel, location, date, and package fields sit directly in the hero - no digging before you can start planning.

→ Goal 01 · Booking conversion
02

Explore by theme

Honeymoon, Adventure, Family, Religious, Water Activities, and Weekends replace one long undifferentiated destination list.

→ Hypothesis 01 · Intent-based nav
03

Real Travelers, Real Stories

Named travelers, trip photos, and star ratings sit right on the homepage - not buried in a blog.

→ Hypothesis 03 · Social proof
04

"Why Choose Us" trust strip

Sanitized premises, trusted advisors, safe & secure, 24/7 support - addressing the credibility gap travelers named directly.

→ Goal 04 · Platform credibility
